About the Challenge

The IEEE Smart World Challenge is an international hackathon bringing together 35 selected participants to tackle a research challenge focused on AI for smart cities and next-gen technologies, including digital twins.

Over two days, participants will work in multidisciplinary teams, supported by academics, researchers and experts from the IEEE community, with the goal of transforming a research challenge into a concrete solution.

Why Take Part

The Smart World Challenge offers the opportunity to experience the IEEE Smart World Congress from an active perspective, contributing to a collaborative design experience that brings together research, technology and expertise from different backgrounds.

The Challenge

AI for Smart Cities & Next-Gen Technologies (including Digital Twins)

The challenge will focus on AI for Smart Cities and Next-Gen Technologies, including Digital Twins; an area exploring how artificial intelligence can drive smarter, more adaptive and responsive urban systems and services. Participants will be invited to design innovative solutions that explore how AI-driven technologies, including digital twins, can support smarter, more adaptive and intelligent systems in real-world scenarios.

The detailed challenge statement will be presented at the opening of the event.
