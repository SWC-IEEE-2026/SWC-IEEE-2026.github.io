Historic Venue & Cultural Landmark

Villa Rendano: History, Elegance & Panoramic Beauty

The IEEE SWC 2026 Social Event will be celebrated in the magnificent setting of Villa Rendano (villarendano.it ↗), one of the most prestigious historic residences in Cosenza, Calabria.

Built in 1891 by the world-renowned concert pianist and composer Alfonso Rendano (inventor of the independent third pedal on the piano), the villa stands proudly atop the scenic Colle Triglio, overlooking the historical center of Cosenza, the convergence of the Crati and Busento rivers, and the Norman-Swabian Castle.

Characterized by a refined Tuscan and Florentine Renaissance Revival architectural style, the residence boasts splendid frescoed ceilings, Pompeian decorative motifs, an imposing grand staircase, and the legendary Sala della Musica. Lovingly preserved and revitalized by the Fondazione Attilio ed Elena Giuliani, Villa Rendano is today both a vibrant cultural center and home to Consentia Itinera, a cutting-edge multimedia museum honoring the rich cultural heritage of Cosenza.
