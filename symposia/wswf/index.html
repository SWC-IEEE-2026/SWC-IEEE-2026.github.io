Join Us at the IEEE Women in the Smart World Symposium

The Women in the Smart World Symposium is a premier platform exploring the role of women in shaping and thriving in the rapidly evolving landscape of smart technology and innovation in an era defined by Artificial Intelligence, the Internet of Things, and Cyber-Physical-Social Systems. Gender diversity is not just a matter of equity but a catalyst for more efficient, sustainable, and human-centric technological solutions. Whether you are an established professional, a seasoned or emerging researcher, an entrepreneur, a policymaker, or a student beginning your journey, the IEEE Women in the Smart World Symposium offers an inspiring environment to learn from leading voices, showcase innovative work, exchange experiences, and develop meaningful partnerships. Together, we will explore how diverse perspectives can shape a smarter, more equitable, and more sustainable future for all.

Discussions will examine how women are leading innovation across STEM disciplines, overcoming barriers to participation and leadership, fostering entrepreneurship, and creating smart systems that improve the quality of life through applications in healthcare, education, sustainable communities, and public services. Special attention will be given to designing technologies that reflect the diversity of society by considering gender, race, age, disability, geography, and other lived experiences, ensuring that the benefits of the Smart World are accessible to everyone.

Participants will also explore the future of responsible and inclusive intelligence through conversations on ethical AI, cybersecurity, generative AI, digital education, mentorship, and the development of resilient smart infrastructures.

Topics

Topics may include, but are not limited to:

  • Women enabling smart world leadership – STEM, barriers and solutions
  • Smart world entrepreneurship – platforms, case studies, smart systems
  • Inclusive intelligence – ethics, cybersecurity
  • Advancing digital futures – AI, smart networks, intelligent technologies
  • Women, health, and ubiquitous smart technologies
  • Designing smart cities – gender-aware modelling, digital twins, and public service
  • Women, education, and generative AI futures – smart education, mentorship
  • Bridging a smart, connected world – gender, race, age, disability, geography

Meet the organizing leadership of the 2026 Women in the Smart World Symposium.

IEEE Steering Committee
Dr. Runhe Huang

Hosei University,
Deputy Director of Hosei Library, Japan

Biography

Runhe Huang earned her Ph.D. in Computer Science and Mathematics in the UK in 1993. After seven years at the University of Aizu, she joined Hosei University, Japan, in 2000. Becoming a full professor in 2003, she has led the Laboratory of Artificial Intelligence. She chaired the Computer Science Department from 2008 to 2010 and served as chair and vice-chair for the IEEE Computational Intelligence Society's Smart World Technical Committee from 2019 to 2022.

IEEE Executive Chair
Dr. Binbin Zhou

Associate Professor, Hangzhou City University, China

Biography

Binbin Zhou, Associate Professor at Hangzhou City University, Deputy Director of the Institute of Computer Systems Engineering, Deputy Director of the Zhejiang Provincial International Scientific Research Cooperation Base. Her main research areas include brain-computer intelligence, multimodal representation, artificial intelligence and urban computing. She has presided projects supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and Zhejiang Provincial Natural Science Foundation and participated in several National Natural Science Foundation and National Key R&D Program projects. She has published over 50 academic papers in prestigious journals and conferences including ACM, IEEE Transactions, and top CCF-A conferences such as CVPR, ICML and IJCAI.

IEEE General Chair
Dr. Rosy Xi Luo

CEO, Raygen Innovation

Biography

Dr. Rosy Luo is CEO of Raygen Innovation and an NVIDIA Omniverse Community Ambassador. She leads an international community advancing AI innovation among women and young builders. She has co-organized programs with Google EAC and Google Cloud on empowering women and young builders from idea to product. She also served as a panelist at the 2024 NVIDIA Inception Showcase Shetech Panel on women's leadership in the new industrial revolution, alongside Maggie Liu (NVIDIA Global Vice President) and Shen Ye (Temasek China Vice President).

IEEE General Chair
Dr. Lisa Carter

Associate Professor, Athabasca University, Canada

Biography

Lisa Carter earned a PhD in Science from the University of New South Wales, Australia. She is a member of the Faculty of Science and Technology (FST) at Athabasca University, Canada, and has held several leadership roles, most recently serving over two five-year terms as the inaugural Dean of FST. She has also led provincial and national organizations dedicated to advancing women in STEM. As a Board member for Alberta Science and Technology, she supports the recognition and promotion of excellence in science and technology among researchers, entrepreneurs, and innovators. Throughout her career, she has championed building opportunities for future generations in STEM.
