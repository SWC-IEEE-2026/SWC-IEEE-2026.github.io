Session 1 · Keynote Address

Exploring the Universe in a Male-Dominated Environment

Prof. Sandra Savaglio · University of Calabria, Italy

Thursday, September 10, 2026
14:30 — 15:30
Room: Aula Magna
{% include wswf-nav.html active="keynote" %}

Abstract

Astrophysics is a highly competitive and truly global discipline, where the most ambitious achievements are possible only through international collaboration and cutting-edge research infrastructures. Building a career as a woman in such a traditionally male-dominated environment is far from easy. Drawing on my experience across Europe and the United States, I will share a personal perspective, including some unexpected and encouraging experiences along the way. The key lesson I have learned is that resilience and inclusion are essential to building a better future for all.

Keynote Speaker

Prof. Sandra Savaglio

Professor, Astrophysics, Cosmology, and Space Science
University of Calabria, Rende, Italy

Biography

Prof. Sandra Savaglio is a multiple international award-winning Italian astrophysicist and professor whose research explores the evolution of galaxies, the young universe, and gamma-ray bursts. She has held research positions at leading international institutions such as the Space Telescope Science Institute and Johns Hopkins University, Baltimore and Germany (European Southern Observatory and Max-Planck Institute for Extraterrestrial Physics, Munich). She is recognized for advancing our understanding of galaxy formation through observations with some of the world's most powerful telescopes. She was featured on the cover of Time magazine in 2004. Beyond her scientific contributions, she is a strong advocate for increasing the visibility and participation of women in science, inspiring future generations through research, mentorship, and public engagement. Prof. Savaglio has served on Councils, such as the Scientific Council of the National Institute of Astrophysics. Since 2024, she is a member of the Scientific Technical Council of the Italian Space Agency.
